Dreamscape Clouds Theme brings a quiet sky to Chrome. A soft lilac frame wraps the top of the window, the toolbar and active tab cool into icy aqua, and the new tab page opens on pale blue. Deep blue-violet text keeps tab titles, bookmarks, the address bar and toolbar icons clear, while deep lilac marks the new tab header and links. Flat solid colors throughout — no wallpaper, no textures, no gradients, no permissions.
