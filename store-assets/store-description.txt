NAME
Dreamscape Clouds Theme

SUMMARY
A dreamy pastel Chrome theme blending soft lilac, airy blue, and icy aqua for a calm, cloud-inspired browsing space.

DESCRIPTION
Dreamscape Clouds Theme brings a quiet sky to Chrome. A soft pastel lilac frame pairs with an icy aqua toolbar and active tab, while a pale blue new tab page keeps your browsing space light and calm. Deep blue-violet text and icons keep the interface clear and easy to read.

Inspired by drifting clouds and open skies, this minimal theme uses solid colors throughout. Its flat crescent, cloud and star icon brings a small touch of dreaminess. No wallpaper, no extra scripts, and no permissions required.
